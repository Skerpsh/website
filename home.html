About Us

Rheinmetall is a global technology group with over 130 years of engineering excellence. Founded in 1889 and headquartered in Düsseldorf, Germany, the company stands at the forefront of defense, security, and industrial innovation.

With a workforce of more than 25,000 professionals and operations in 30+ countries, Rheinmetall delivers advanced solutions that meet the highest standards of precision, reliability, and performance. The company is a trusted partner to governments and industries worldwide, supporting armed forces and civil sectors with cutting-edge technologies.

Rheinmetall’s portfolio spans armored vehicles, weapon systems, ammunition, electronic solutions, and digital security technologies, combining decades of experience with continuous innovation. Significant investments in research and development ensure readiness for the challenges of tomorrow, including automation, artificial intelligence, and next-generation defense systems.

Driven by responsibility and sustainability, Rheinmetall actively integrates environmental and ethical standards into its operations while advancing dual-use technologies for both military and civilian applications.
